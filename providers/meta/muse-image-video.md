# Muse Image and Muse Video `[provider-doc]`

*Last reviewed: 2026-08-06.*

## At a glance

| Model | Role | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Muse Image | Image generation and editing | Available in Meta AI and selected Meta product surfaces |
| Muse Video | Video generation with native audio | Preview; not yet generally available |

Official announcement: [Introducing Muse Image and Muse Video](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-image-muse-video-msl/).

## What they are

Muse Image and Muse Video are media-generation models from Meta Superintelligence Labs. Muse Image supports generation, editing, multi-reference composition, and tool-assisted refinement. Muse Video shares the pretraining base and adds video generation with native audio.

## Availability

Muse Image is available in the Meta AI app and on `meta.ai`, with selected availability in Instagram Stories and WhatsApp. Muse Video remains an early preview and is described as coming to creators and Meta AI.

The launch announcement describes product availability, not a public developer API or downloadable weights. Treat Muse Image and Muse Video as Meta-hosted product surfaces unless Meta publishes a separate developer or weight release.
