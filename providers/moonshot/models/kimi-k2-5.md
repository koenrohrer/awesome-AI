# Kimi K2.5 `[provider-doc]`

*Last reviewed: 2026-08-16.*

## At a glance

- Provider: Moonshot AI
- Model ID: `kimi-k2.5`
- Tier: Earlier multimodal generalist
- Context window: 256K tokens
- Hosted status: Still listed as active, but closed to newly registered accounts. The model list states that after the Kimi K3 launch, `kimi-k2.5` and the `moonshot-v1` series are no longer available to newly registered users, with a full platform sunset on August 31
- Official docs: [model list](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/models), [use the Kimi vision model](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/guide/use-kimi-vision-model)

## What it is

Kimi K2.5 is an earlier Moonshot multimodal model with text and visual input, thinking and non-thinking modes, and explicit agent-task positioning. [Model list](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/models).

The platform no longer publishes a dedicated K2.5 pricing page; the pricing index covers Kimi K3, Kimi K2.7 Code, Kimi K2.6, and Moonshot V1. [Pricing](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/pricing/chat).

## Migration

For new work, use [Kimi K3](kimi-k3.md), [Kimi K2.7 Code](kimi-k2-7-code.md), or [Kimi K2.6](kimi-k2-6.md). K2.6 is the closest replacement for K2.5's toggleable thinking and 256K context.

Open weights for K2.5 remain on [Moonshot's Hugging Face org](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai) and are a separate surface from hosted availability; check the model card and license before self-hosting.

## Public system card availability

Moonshot does not publish a public system-card page for K2.5.
