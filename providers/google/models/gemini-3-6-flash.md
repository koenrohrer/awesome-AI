## What it is

Gemini 3.6 Flash is Google's stable fast model for multimodal and agentic workloads. The model page lists thinking, caching, Batch, Flex, and Priority inference support in addition to the tools above.

Computer Use remains a preview capability even though the underlying model is stable. Google recommends supervision and a sandbox for UI-control tasks.
