## What it is

Gemini Omni Flash is Google's conversational video-generation and editing preview. It accepts text and images for generation, or video clips up to 10 seconds for editing, and supports iterative natural-language refinement through the Interactions API.

The model detail and pricing pages use the preview ID above. The broader models index also lists the `gemini-omni-flash` endpoint name, so verify the accepted ID in the target API surface before deployment.
