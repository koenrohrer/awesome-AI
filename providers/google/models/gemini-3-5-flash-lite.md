## What it is

Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is Google's stable low-cost 3.5 model for high-throughput agent tasks, document parsing, and extraction. It supports Batch, Flex, and Priority inference as separate consumption tiers.

The standard price above excludes grounding requests and cache storage. Check the live pricing page before building a cost model.
